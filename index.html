04 / Questions

Before you ask.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. After the free audit you get one fixed price, so the quote you approve is the amount you pay.

How long does a project take?

A business website usually takes one to two weeks. Apps, EPOS and clinic systems take longer and are delivered in stages you can test.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes. Most of our website and ads work is for businesses in the UK and UAE. Everything is handled over WhatsApp and email.

Can you run ads for a site you did not build?

Yes. We check the landing page first, because sending paid clicks to a slow or unclear page wastes your budget.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

You get a reply on WhatsApp with a short audit or a few questions about what you need, then a quote.

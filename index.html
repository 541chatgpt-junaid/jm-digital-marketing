05 / Questions

Before you ask.

How much does a website cost?

A standard business website is £150 one-off. That covers the full build, plus your domain and hosting for the first year. From year two, domain and hosting renewal is payable yearly. There is nothing monthly unless you choose an ongoing growth plan.

How long does a project take?

Most standard websites are ready in 2 days once we have your details — your services, photos and logo, or we can work from your public listings and social pages. Larger or custom builds take longer, and we'll tell you the honest timescale before we start.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes. Most of our website and ads work is for businesses in the UK and UAE. Everything is handled over WhatsApp and email.

Can you run ads for a site you did not build?

Yes. We check the landing page first, because sending paid clicks to a slow or unclear page wastes your budget.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

You get a reply on WhatsApp with a short audit or a few questions about what you need, then a quote.
